Account support

Delete your WalkChamp account

Submit an account deletion request from WalkChamp or use the secure request form below if you no longer have access to the app.

Request deletion in WalkChamp

  1. Open WalkChamp.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Select Request Account Deletion and follow the confirmation steps.

Request deletion without the app

Enter the email address associated with your account. Your request will be delivered to admin@miragaming.com. We may ask you to verify account ownership before completing the request.

Before you delete

  • Withdraw any eligible cash balance before requesting deletion.
  • Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
  • Some payment, tax, fraud-prevention, dispute, or other legal records may be retained for the period required by applicable law.
For WalkChamp account-deletion questions, contact admin@miragaming.com.
